# System of a Downs -- Universe Map & Location Compendium

Visual reference descriptions optimized for text-to-image generation and AI map generation.

## UNIVERSE STRUCTURE

The galaxy is organized into 4 major regions, with Hong Kong existing as a bridge location between two of them.

```
THE CORE TRANSIT WORLDS (clean, sterile, corporate)
├── Antwerp
├── Hong Kong ← BRIDGE LOCATION (between Core and Sailor Town)
│
SAILOR TOWN / THE DOCKING CULTURE BELT (warm, loud, emotional, lived-in)
├── Sailor Town (the heart)
├── Liverpool
├── Tiger Bay
├── Frisco Town
│
THE FRONTIER BELTS (dangerous, improvised, labor-intensive)
├── Port Adelaide
├── Valapo
├── Dunkirk
│
THE QUANTUM HIGHWAYS (transit infrastructure, ancient, industrial-beautiful)
├── The Quantum Highways (the routes themselves)
├── Port Mahon (waypoint/hub along the highway system)
├── Off-Road Space (the voids beyond the highways)
```

### The Quantum Highways

## BRIDGE LOCATION

---

### Hong Kong

**Location Type:** Bridge (between Core Transit Worlds and Sailor Town)
**Image:** Flux2-Klein_00020_.png

**Visual Description:**
A vertical neon megacity where ships dock directly into skyscrapers. Sits in the liminal space between Core efficiency and Sailor Town grit -- infrastructure that works aggressively, but the commerce is gray-market, favor-based, and personal. Everyone is selling information, access, favors, or secrets. The systems are polished but the culture is not. Too fast and too commercial for the Core to fully claim it, too organized and too vertical for Sailor Town to absorb it. It exists in the gap between both, belonging to neither.

**Image Prompt Keywords:**
Vertical neon megacity, ships docking into skyscrapers, cyberpunk-inspired architecture, dense holographic signage, elevated walkways between towers, information broker dens, floating karaoke bars, market stalls at every level, blue and pink neon, rain-slicked surfaces, hyper-commercial atmosphere, data trading floors.

**Atmosphere:**
Fast, overwhelming, exciting, dangerous. Everyone is selling something. If you can't buy it here, it probably doesn't exist.

**Famous Landmark:**
A floating karaoke bar operated by The Sing-Song Girl -- an information broker whose name is both a cover and a brand.

## MAP GENERATION NOTES

**For AI map generation, key spatial relationships:**

1. The Core Transit Worlds sit at the CENTER -- the most connected, most infrastructure-dense region
2. The Quantum Highways radiate OUTWARD from the Core like arterial roads
3. Sailor Town / Docking Culture Belt exists along the highways -- populated waypoints where culture accumulated
4. Hong Kong sits at the BORDER between Core and Sailor Town -- visually it should overlap both zones
5. The Frontier Belts are at the EDGES -- far from Core, poorly connected, at the ends of highways or off them entirely
6. Off-Road Space is BETWEEN everything -- the dark gaps between highways where nothing is managed
7. Port Mahon sits ON a major highway junction -- a waypoint hub

**Scale suggestion:** The Core is small and dense. The Highways are long and thin. Sailor Town locations cluster along highway routes. Frontier locations are scattered at the periphery. Off-Road Space fills all the gaps between.
