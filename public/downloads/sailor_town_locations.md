# System of a Downs -- Location Compendium

Visual reference descriptions optimized for text-to-image generation.

---

## Sailor Town

**Visual Description:**
A permanent docking station that accidentally became civilization. Everything feels damp, loud, overused, patched together, and emotionally alive. Old neon signs flicker above repair shops, taverns, and cargo exchanges. Warm and uneven lighting. Music leaks from everywhere. The station constantly groans mechanically. Engine coolant condensation on walls, recycled air visible in shafts of light, fried food stalls with steam rising, machine grease stains on walkways, cheap alcohol signage, salt-like atmospheric scrubber residue on surfaces.

**Image Prompt Keywords:**
Lived-in space station interior, damp metal corridors, flickering neon tavern signs, cargo exchange markets, warm orange lighting, industrial patching, exposed pipes and ductwork, spacer memorial walls, transit tattoo parlors, crowded narrow passages, steam and condensation, working-class science fiction, maritime-inspired space culture.

**Atmosphere:**
Warm, chaotic, emotionally alive. A place that smells like engine coolant, fried food, and cheap alcohol. Nobody notices the mechanical groaning anymore.

**Cultural Details:**
Ship bells, docking chants, sailor superstitions, memorial walls with names of lost ships, transit tattoos, old route songs performed in taverns. Cultural values: reliability, labor, reputation, favors, storytelling, survival.

## Liverpool

**Visual Description:**
Rain-soaked docking piers beneath glowing tavern windows. Warm, traditional, and story-driven culture. The heart of old spacer culture. Wet metal pier grating reflecting warm golden light from pub windows above. Heavy rain falling through dock lights. Traditional wooden-framed tavern interiors visible through foggy glass. Ships moored alongside ancient stone-and-metal quays.

**Image Prompt Keywords:**
Rain-soaked space docks, glowing tavern windows, wet metal piers, warm golden interior light spilling outside, fog and rain, traditional pub architecture in space, moored freight ships, cobblestone-inspired walkways, maritime heritage, cozy harbor atmosphere, The Gull & Lantern tavern.

**Atmosphere:**
Nostalgic, warm, welcoming. The closest thing to coming home. Rain is constant. Stories are currency.

**Famous Landmark:**
The Gull & Lantern -- a tavern where spacer stories are told, crews are recruited, and old traditions are maintained.

## Port Adelaide

**Visual Description:**
Massive drydocks carved into canyon cliffs beneath permanent orange skies. Thousands of half-disassembled ships hang above the harbor on scaffolding and crane systems. Gruff blue-collar spacer culture. Sparks from welding torches falling like rain. Industrial cranes silhouetted against burnt-orange atmosphere. Canyon walls with docking bays cut directly into rock face.

**Image Prompt Keywords:**
Canyon drydocks, orange sky atmosphere, massive ship scaffolding, half-disassembled vessels hanging from cranes, industrial welding sparks, cliff-face docking bays, heavy machinery, shipbreaking yards, reactor recycling facilities, blue-collar workers in coveralls, industrial science fiction, rust and metal textures.

**Atmosphere:**
Industrial, honest, hardworking. A place that respects mechanics, welders, salvagers, and anyone who can keep a ship alive.

**Economy:**
Shipbreaking, reactor recycling, aftermarket ship parts, emergency dock repair, black-market engineering.

## Hong Kong

**Visual Description:**
A vertical neon megacity where ships dock directly into skyscrapers. Fast-moving and hyper-commercial. Endless vertical neon signage in multiple languages. Ships threading between towers. Elevated walkways connecting buildings at multiple levels. Data screens and holographic advertisements everywhere. Dense, layered, overwhelming.

**Image Prompt Keywords:**
Vertical neon megacity, ships docking into skyscrapers, cyberpunk-inspired architecture, dense holographic signage, elevated walkways between towers, information broker dens, floating karaoke bars, market stalls at every level, blue and pink neon, rain-slicked surfaces, hyper-commercial atmosphere, data trading floors.

**Atmosphere:**
Fast, overwhelming, exciting, dangerous. Everyone is selling something. If you can't buy it here, it probably doesn't exist.

**Famous Landmark:**
A floating karaoke bar operated by The Sing-Song Girl -- an information broker whose name is both a cover and a brand.

## Tiger Bay

**Visual Description:**
Floating taverns and narrow alleys overflowing with music. Emotional, expressive, and deeply social culture. Interconnected floating platforms with warm interior lighting. Musicians performing on every corner. Narrow alley passages between establishments draped with colored fabric and string lights.

**Image Prompt Keywords:**
Floating tavern platforms, narrow alleys with string lights, live music venues, warm amber lighting, colored fabric canopies, crowded social spaces, musicians performing, barter market stalls, contraband shipping docks, expressive emotional atmosphere, intimate scale architecture.

**Atmosphere:**
Musical, emotional, alive. If Tiger Bay knows your name, you are either famous or doomed.

**Economy:**
Music halls, contraband shipping, barter markets, underground repair.

## Antwerp

**Visual Description:**
Perfectly symmetrical docking towers stretching endlessly into orbit. Professional, efficient, and emotionally sterile culture. Clean geometric lines. Identical tower after tower. Automated cargo systems moving with precision. No decoration, no character, no warmth. Pure function.

**Image Prompt Keywords:**
Symmetrical orbital docking towers, sterile white and gray architecture, automated cargo systems, geometric precision, endless identical structures, administrative offices, customs processing halls, freight regulation centers, emotionally cold lighting, corporate efficiency, bureaucratic architecture.

**Atmosphere:**
Clinical, precise, suffocating. The galaxy's paperwork capital. Everything works perfectly. Nobody smiles.

**Famous Figure:**
The Ledgermaster -- a legendary customs official feared by independent traders galaxy-wide. Has never been bribed, never made an error, never shown mercy on a filing deadline.

## Dunkirk

**Visual Description:**
Fog-covered orbital docks surrounded by endless gray seas. Old warships still float near the harbor walls. The culture is quiet, tired, and resilient. Thick fog obscuring visibility beyond a few hundred meters. Ghostly silhouettes of decommissioned warships. Gray water below, gray sky above. Small tavern lights barely visible through the mist.

**Image Prompt Keywords:**
Fog-covered orbital docks, endless gray seas, silhouettes of old warships, mist and low visibility, tired resilient architecture, refugee dock infrastructure, humanitarian aid stations, quiet somber atmosphere, tavern lights through fog, memorial markers, medical ship heritage.

**Atmosphere:**
Quiet, tired, resilient. The place ships run to when nowhere else will take them.

**Famous Landmark:**
Sweet Marie's tavern -- the oldest tavern on the refugee docks. Neutral ground where exhausted people can rest without politics.

**Image Prompt Keywords:**
Carnival lights on industrial infrastructure, casino signage, performance hall facades, chaotic neon lighting, aging metal structures, entertainment district, gambling dens, smoky interiors, dramatic stage lighting, burlesque-era inspired space architecture, excess and decay.

## Valapo

**Visual Description:**
Dust storms, low gravity, and half-built docking towers. Independent and survival-focused culture at the edge of civilized space. Red-brown dust obscuring half-constructed infrastructure. Low gravity causing dust to hang suspended in air. Sparse settlement buildings hunkered against wind. Fuel refinery flares visible on the horizon.

**Image Prompt Keywords:**
Dust storms, low gravity environment, half-built docking towers, frontier settlement, red-brown atmosphere, fuel refinery flares on horizon, sparse architecture, survival infrastructure, ore extraction equipment, wind-worn buildings, edge of civilization, harsh frontier landscape.

**Atmosphere:**
Harsh, independent, frontier. The edge of civilized space. Leadership shifts constantly between mining crews, mercenary outfits, and fuel cartels.

**Economy:**
Ore extraction, fuel refining, survival trade, scavenging, frontier supply transport.

Ceremonial flags and naval insignia. Well-maintained docking facilities with military precision. Veterans walking the promenades.

**Image Prompt Keywords:**
Retired warships as monuments, naval memorial harbor, ceremonial flags and insignia, disciplined architecture, military precision docking facilities, veteran promenades, ceremonial shipyards, dignified atmosphere, traditional naval architecture, monument lighting, brass and steel.

**Atmosphere:**
Dignified, traditional, slightly bitter. The old fleet never truly retired.

**Economy:**
Naval maintenance, military archives, officer pensions, ceremonial shipyards.

## The Core Transit Worlds

**Visual Description:**
Clean, sterile, optimized, and emotionally distant. Everything works. Perfectly. White transit corridors, automated logistics towers, synchronized traffic lanes, AI-managed infrastructure, enormous orbital elevators, quiet crowds, predictive advertisements, immaculate docking systems. No visible repairs anywhere -- systems are replaced before deterioration becomes visible.

**Image Prompt Keywords:**
Sterile white transit corridors, automated logistics towers, synchronized traffic lanes, orbital elevators, AI-managed infrastructure, predictive holographic advertisements, immaculate surfaces, no visible wear, emotionally cold perfection, corporate architecture, efficient crowds, transactional atmosphere.

**Atmosphere:**
Perfect. Efficient. Empty. This is where Reginal originates culturally. The philosophical opposite of The Wayward Naught's design philosophy.

**Population:**
Administrators, transit analysts, corporate logistics workers, infrastructure engineers, policy officials. People move quickly and speak efficiently. Conversations feel transactional.

**Visual Description:**
Dangerous, improvised, and economically fragile. Mining scaffolds, rotating habitat drums, exposed industrial machinery, cracked pressure plating, jury-rigged oxygen systems, massive refinery flares, dust-covered settlements. Everything looks temporary -- some of it has existed for generations.

**Image Prompt Keywords:**
Mining scaffolds in space, rotating habitat drums, exposed industrial machinery, cracked pressure plating, jury-rigged life support, massive refinery flares, dust-covered settlements, improvised infrastructure, temporary structures that became permanent, harsh industrial lighting, survival architecture, economic desperation.

**Atmosphere:**
Harsh, desperate, resilient. Civilization exists here only because labor keeps it alive manually. Systems fail often. People adapt constantly.

**Population:**
Miners, salvagers, refinery crews, migrant workers, independent contractors, displaced populations, black-market mechanics.

## The Quantum Highways

**Visual Description:**
Artificial rivers connecting civilization. Glowing transit lanes, massive navigation beacons, gravitational anchor structures, ancient maintenance stations, endless streams of cargo traffic, AI signal arrays stretching into darkness. Less "places" and more infrastructure at incomprehensible scale.

**Image Prompt Keywords:**
Glowing transit lanes stretching to infinity, massive navigation beacons, gravitational anchor structures, ancient maintenance stations, cargo traffic streams, AI signal arrays in darkness, eerie beautiful light, industrial scale beyond comprehension, ancient technology, cosmic infrastructure, lonely maintenance outposts.

**Atmosphere:**
Eerie, beautiful, industrial, ancient, incomprehensibly massive. Most people treat them like weather. Nobody fully understands the entire system anymore.

**Folklore:**
Ghost signals, dead routes, phantom distress calls, cursed lanes, drifting ships, ancient automated stations still functioning alone. Many spacer songs revolve around Highway travel.

## Off-Road Space

**Visual Description:**
The voids beyond stable transit infrastructure. Dark voids beyond mapped lanes, dead satellites, abandoned stations, signal silence, unstable gravitational regions, drifting wreckage. Navigation here is dangerous, slow, unpredictable, and economically irrational. The galaxy feels genuinely enormous.

**Image Prompt Keywords:**
Deep space void, no transit infrastructure, dead satellites drifting, abandoned stations in darkness, signal silence, unstable gravitational distortion, drifting wreckage fields, absolute darkness punctuated by distant stars, isolation, existential scale, ancient forgotten structures, cosmic loneliness.

**Atmosphere:**
Silent, ancient, terrifying, vast. This is where the universe stops feeling managed and starts feeling ancient. Spacer folklore becomes strongest here. Something ancient may exist in the voids.
